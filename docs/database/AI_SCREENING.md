# Overview

The AI Screening module provides preliminary canine skin disease screening using an on-device TensorFlow Lite model.

The AI Screening module assists veterinarians.

It never replaces veterinarian diagnosis.

Screening may be performed by:

- Pet Owner (Mobile Application)
- Veterinarian (Web Application)

AI Screening records become part of the pet's medical history.

---

# Entity

## DISEASE

Purpose

Stores all supported canine skin diseases recognized by the AI model.

Using a lookup table standardizes prediction names.

# Entity

## AI_SCREENING

Purpose

Stores every AI prediction generated by the mobile application.

AI Screening exists independently of Consultations.

If the owner later visits a clinic, the screening may be linked to a Consultation.

---

## Attributes

| Column | Type | Constraints |
|---------|------|-------------|
| AIS_ID | UUID | PK |
| PET_ID | UUID | FK → PET |
| DIS_ID | UUID | FK → DISEASE |
| CON_ID | UUID | FK → CONSULTATION, NULL |
| USR_ID | UUID | FK → USER |
| AIS_CONFIDENCE | DECIMAL(5,2) | NOT NULL |
| AIS_MODEL_VERSION | VARCHAR(50) | NOT NULL |
| AIS_INFERENCE_TIME_MS | INTEGER | NULL |
| AIS_DEVICE | VARCHAR(100) | NULL |
| AIS_STATUS | ENUM | NOT NULL |
| AIS_CREATED_AT | TIMESTAMP | DEFAULT NOW() |

---

# Screening Status

PENDING_REVIEW

REVIEWED

CONFIRMED

DISMISSED

---

# Business Rules

Every AI Screening belongs to one Pet.

AI Screening may exist without a Consultation.

Only the Pet Owner who owns the Pet may create AI Screenings.

Veterinarians may review AI results.

AI predictions are immutable.

Model version must always be recorded.

# Entity

## AI_SCREENING_IMAGE

Purpose

Stores every image submitted for AI inference.

Multiple images may belong to one AI Screening.

---

---

# Image Type

ORIGINAL

CROPPED

PREPROCESSED

ANNOTATED

---

# Business Rules

Every AI Screening must contain at least one ORIGINAL image.

Additional processed images are optional.

Images cannot be deleted once a Consultation references the AI Screening.

---

# Validation Rules

Confidence must be between 0 and 100.

Only active pets may be screened.

Only the Pet Owner may submit images for their own pets.

Only Veterinarians may change AI Screening status.
