# Overview

The Notification module manages all system-generated notifications delivered to users.

Notifications may be delivered to:

- Pet Owners
- Receptionists
- Veterinarians
- Clinic Administrators
- Super Administrators

Notifications improve communication between pet owners and veterinary clinics.

---

# Entity

## NOTIFICATION

Purpose

Stores notifications generated by the XPawSure system.

Notifications are read-only records that inform users about important events.

# Business Rules

Every Notification belongs to exactly one User.

Users may only read their own notifications.

Notifications cannot be modified.

Notifications may be marked as read.

Notifications should never be permanently deleted.
